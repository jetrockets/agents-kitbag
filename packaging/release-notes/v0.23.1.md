## New

- **Check for updates.** Settings, About now says how the last look for a newer version ended (this is the newest, a version is available, or why it could not check) and has a button to look again. The app still looks by itself when it starts and every six hours.

## Fixed

- **Each update left about 60 MB behind**, in a hidden folder beside the app, holding the downloaded package and a copy of the previous version. The app now removes what finished updates left, the next time it starts.
- **A locked 1Password looked like a missing token.** When 1Password listed its vaults but would not hand over the token (it locked in between, or the prompt was dismissed), the check said "token missing". It now says that 1Password did not hand it over.

## Updating

Copies of 0.23.0 will offer this version in the sidebar: "Restart to update". This is the first update checked against the signing key compiled into the app.
